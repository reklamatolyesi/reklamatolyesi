Points to Consider in Totem Design

An effective totem design should not only be aesthetically pleasing but also functional and eye-catching. Here are some important points to consider in totem design:

  • Visual Impact: It is important that your totem is visible from a distance and memorable.
  • Readability: The text and logos on it must be easily readable.
  • Material Quality: Durable, long-lasting materials that are weather-resistant should be preferred.
  • Color Harmony: Colors that reflect your brand and are harmonious should be used.
  • Positioning: Placing your totem in the correct location will increase its visibility.
