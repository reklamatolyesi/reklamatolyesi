Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How Long Do LED Letterbox Signs Last?

With quality materials and proper installation, LED letterbox signs can last for many years. The lifespan of the LEDs is typically between 50,000 and 100,000 hours.

How Do You Maintain LED Letterbox Signs?

Maintaining LED letterbox signs is generally easy. Regular cleaning and checking the LEDs is sufficient. If any malfunctions occur, you can contact our Advertising Workshop technical team for support.

What Materials Are Used for Letterbox Signs?

Acrylic, aluminum composite panel (ACP), stainless steel, and acrylic are commonly used in letterbox signs. Material selection may vary depending on the sign’s area of use and design.
