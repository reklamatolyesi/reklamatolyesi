Cookie Policy

This policy explains the cookies used on the reklamatolyesi.org website operated by Gaziantep Tabela ve Reklam Hizmetleri, the purposes for which they are used and how you can manage your preferences. Personal data obtained through cookies is processed in accordance with Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data.

What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device through your browser when you visit a website. They serve purposes such as maintaining your session, remembering your preferences and understanding how the website is used. Cookies may be session cookies, which are deleted when the browser is closed, or persistent cookies, which remain on the device for a certain period.

Types of cookies we use

The following categories of cookies may be used on our website:

  • Essential cookies: Required to provide the basic functions, security and session of the website; disabling them may prevent the website from working.
  • Functional cookies: Offer a more personal experience by remembering your preferences such as language, region or display.
  • Analytics cookies: Help improve the website by measuring, in aggregate, how visitors use it.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to show content and advertisements suited to your interests.

Consent and managing your preferences

Cookies other than essential cookies are used only with your explicit consent. You can set your preferences through the cookie notice shown on your first visit to our website, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

You can also view, delete or block cookies through the privacy or site data options in your browser settings. If you block cookies completely, some parts of the website may not work as expected.

Your rights

To exercise your rights under Article 11 of the Law concerning your personal data processed through cookies, you may apply via info@reklamatolyesi.org.
