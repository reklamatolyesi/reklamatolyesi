Vehicle Wrapping Options

As Reklam Atölyesi, we offer various vehicle wrapping options in Gaziantep to meet different needs.

Full Vehicle Wrapping

This option, which covers the entire vehicle, is the most effective advertising solution. Your brand's logo, slogan, and other visual elements are applied to every surface of your vehicle. Especially in a crowded city like Gaziantep, it maximizes your brand's visibility.

Partial Vehicle Wrapping

This option, which covers specific areas of your vehicle, is a more economical solution. It is usually applied to areas such as doors, fenders, and the hood. It offers a budget-friendly advertising alternative for businesses in Gaziantep.

Transparent Vehicle Wrapping

It protects your vehicle’s original color while providing protection against scratches and other external factors. It is an ideal option especially for luxury vehicles. It is a perfect solution for those who want to protect the value of their vehicle in Gaziantep.

Folio Print Vehicle Wrapping

This is one of the most preferred methods in Gaziantep. Designs prepared with high-resolution digital printing technology are applied to the folio and specially adhered to your vehicle.

How to Maintain Vehicle Wrapping?

To extend the life of your vehicle wrapping applied in Gaziantep, it is important to follow some simple maintenance tips:

  • Wash your vehicle regularly.
  • Avoid hard brushes and abrasive cleaning agents.
  • Stay away from sharp objects to prevent damage to the wrapping.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to sunlight.
