### Banner Types in Gaziantep
  • **Banner (PVC) Banners:** Ideal for outdoor use, weather resistant and suitable for wide format printing.
  • **Vinyl Banners:** Can be used indoors and outdoors, has bright and vibrant colors.
  • **Mesh Banners:** Ideal for use in windy areas, provides durability with its air-permeable structure.
  • **Roll Up Banners:** Portable and easy to set up, a practical solution for fairs and events.
  • **X Banner Banners:** Similar to roll-ups, portable and frequently preferred at fairs.
