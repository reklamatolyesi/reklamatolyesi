Things to Consider in Label Design

Many factors need to be considered during the label design process. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

  • Brand Identity: Your label design should reflect your brand identity and use a consistent visual language.
  • Target Audience: Your label design should be appropriate for the interests and expectations of your target audience.
  • Product Information: You should clearly and understandably state all necessary information about your product (name, description, contents, instructions for use, etc.) on your label.
  • Visual Design: Your label design should be eye-catching and aesthetically pleasing. Colors, fonts, and visuals should be carefully selected and used harmoniously.
  • Material Quality: Quality materials should be used for your labels to be durable and long-lasting.

What Types of Labels Are Available in Gaziantep?

As Reklam Atölyesi, we offer a wide range of labels to meet the diverse needs of businesses in Gaziantep. Here are some popular label types:

Product Labels

These are labels that are applied to your products and contain information about them. They are frequently used in sectors such as food, cosmetics, and cleaning products. We offer special solutions for local producers in Gaziantep.

Promotional Labels

These are labels used for promotional purposes and generally provide information about discounts, campaigns, or special offers. They are ideal for increasing the impact of your advertising campaigns.

Barcode Labels

These are labels used to facilitate product tracking and inventory management. They are widely used in the retail sector. We have special solutions for markets and stores in Gaziantep.

Warning Labels

These are labels that contain necessary warnings for the safe use of products. Their use is mandatory, especially for products containing hazardous materials.
