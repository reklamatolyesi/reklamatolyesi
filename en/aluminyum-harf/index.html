### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) #### How Long Can Aluminum Letter Signage Be Used?

Aluminum letter signage can be used for many years with proper maintenance and installation. The durable structure of aluminum resists external factors and prevents problems such as fading.

#### How Do You Maintain Aluminum Letter Signage?

Maintaining aluminum letter signage is quite easy. Regularly wiping it with a damp cloth to remove dust will help maintain the letters' original shine. Avoid using harsh chemical cleaners.

#### Can Aluminum Letter Signage Be Illuminated?

Yes, aluminum letter signage can be perfectly illuminated with internal or external lighting. Using LED modules or halogen lamps, you can make your sign look more impressive at night.
