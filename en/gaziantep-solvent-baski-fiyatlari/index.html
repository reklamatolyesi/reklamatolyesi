Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Solvent Printing?

Solvent printing is a printing technique that uses solvent-based inks. These inks are resistant to water and UV rays, which ensures the longevity of the prints. It is ideal for outdoor advertising, vehicle wrapping and other outdoor applications.

What are Solvent Printing Materials?

Various materials such as vinyl, tarpaulin, paper, and foil can be used for solvent printing. The choice of material varies depending on the purpose of the print and environmental conditions.
