How long does it take to prepare a vinyl banner sign?

The preparation time for a sign varies depending on the complexity and size of the design. Generally, we can prepare your sign quickly, adhering to our principle of fast delivery, once the design is approved.

Are vinyl banner signs weather resistant?

Yes, our vinyl banner signs are made from high-quality materials and are weather resistant. However, you should take precautions to prevent damage to your sign in cases of strong winds or storms.
