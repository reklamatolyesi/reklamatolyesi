Things to Consider When Designing Directional Signage

Directional signage design directly affects your company’s image and customer experience. Here are some important points to consider during the design process:

  • Readability: It is important that the sign is easily readable from a distance.
  • Color Selection: Use attention-grabbing colors that are appropriate for your brand colors.
  • Font Type: Choose a legible and modern font.
  • Material Quality: Use durable and long-lasting materials.
  • Placement: Determine the location of the sign correctly, keeping it away from elements that obstruct the view.
