Vehicle Wrapping Options

As Reklam Atölyesi, we offer different vehicle wrapping options in Gaziantep. We help you find the solution that best suits your needs and budget. Full Vehicle Wrap

You can achieve maximum visibility and attention by wrapping the entire vehicle. It can be fully customized with your brand's logo, slogan, and other visual elements.

Partial Vehicle Wrap

Transparent Vehicle Wrap

We apply transparent advertising on top of the vehicle’s original color, preserving it. A preferred option especially for luxury vehicles. Your design creates a striking look without compromising the vehicle's elegance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • How long does it take to complete vehicle advertising/wrapping?

The duration of vehicle advertising/wrapping varies depending on the complexity of the design and the size of the vehicle. It is usually completed on the same day or within a maximum of one business day.

  • Does vehicle wrapping damage the vehicle?

As Reklam Atölyesi, we use quality materials and professional application techniques to wrap your vehicle without causing damage. When you remove the wrap, the original paint of the vehicle remains intact.

  • How long does a vehicle wrap last?

The durability of a vehicle wrap depends on the materials used and maintenance. Typically, it retains its original appearance for 3-5 years. You can extend its life with regular cleaning and maintenance.

  • Can I use my own design?

Of course, you can use your own design. We check if your design is suitable for printing and make any necessary adjustments.
