Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does car wrapping take?

The duration of car wrapping varies depending on the size of your vehicle, the complexity of the design, and the application method. Generally, a full vehicle wrap can be completed in one day.

Does car wrapping damage my car’s paint?

When quality vinyl materials are used and proper application techniques are followed, car wrapping does not damage your car’s paint. In fact, it can protect the paint from scratches and the harmful effects of the sun.

Can I wrap my car myself?

Car wrapping requires experience and expertise. Incorrect application can damage your car’s paint or result in an aesthetically unappealing appearance. Therefore, it is recommended to obtain service from a professional company.

How durable are car wrapping vinyls?

Quality vinyl materials are durable against outdoor conditions and maintain their colors for years. However, the lifespan of the vinyl material can vary depending on usage conditions and maintenance.
