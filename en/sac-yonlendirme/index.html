### How is the lighting of sheet metal signs done?

The lighting of sheet metal signs can be done with internally illuminated (LED) or externally illuminated systems. Internal illuminated systems provide a brighter appearance by emitting light from inside the sign. External illuminated systems illuminate the area around the sign.
