## Professional Corporate Identity Design in Gaziantep

As Reklam Atölyesi (Advertising Workshop), we offer professional corporate identity design services in Gaziantep. Unlike social media design, our focus is on corporate identity services and brand guidelines. While social media designs are focused on digital content based on platforms, corporate identity design encompasses brand identity, visual consistency, and the design of corporate documents. We start with logo design and then design corporate documents such as business cards, letterheads, envelopes, files, and invoices. We create a consistent corporate look by establishing a color palette, typography selection, and design language that reflects your brand identity.

### Corporate Identity Elements

In our corporate identity work, we design logos, business cards, letterheads, envelopes, files, and other corporate materials. We prepare a corporate identity guide to ensure the correct use of your brand across all channels. We bring together the design of all materials to be used in digital and print media under one roof.

We design digital corporate identity elements such as social media templates, email signatures, and presentation templates. We deliver complete packages with printing technical specifications, Pantone color codes, and vector files.
