FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We have answered the most common questions about our production process, delivery times, pricing and warranty. If you cannot find your answer here, message us on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApparrow_forward
01How does the production process work?

The process has four steps: we listen to your needs and carry out a site survey and measurement; we prepare the design and send it for your approval; after approval we produce it in our own workshop; finally our installation team completes the application and hands over the job. We keep you informed at every step.

02How long does delivery take?

Design is usually ready within 1–3 working days. After design approval, production takes 5–7 working days for most jobs; installation within Gaziantep usually takes 3–7 working days depending on the size of the job. We state the exact delivery date in writing in our quote.

03How are prices determined?

Because every job is made to measure, we do not have a fixed price list. The price depends on size, material, lighting, quantity and installation conditions. After the survey and measurement we prepare an itemised written quote; we never start production before you approve it.

04Do you charge for the survey and measurement?

No. Our site survey, measurement and consultancy are free of charge. We inspect the location together, choose the most suitable material and size, and plan your project.

05Do your products come with a warranty?

Yes. The materials we use and our workmanship are covered by a warranty. The warranty period depends on the product type (signage, LED, vehicle wrap, print) and is stated in writing in the quote. We also provide technical support after installation.

06What should I send to get a quote?

Just send a photo of the location, approximate dimensions, your logo if you have one and the product you want via WhatsApp or e-mail. We get back to you Monday to Saturday between 09:00 and 19:00.
