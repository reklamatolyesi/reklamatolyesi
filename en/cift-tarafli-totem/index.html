Points to Consider in Totem Design

An effective totem design should reflect your brand’s identity and convey your message clearly. Here are some important points to consider:

  • Visual Impact: Use high-resolution images and striking colors to make your totem stand out.
  • Readability: Ensure that the text is easily readable. Font selection and sizes are important.
  • Brand Identity: Your totem design should reflect your brand colors, logo, and overall corporate identity.
  • Positioning: Consider the traffic flow and viewing angle of the location where your totem will be placed.
