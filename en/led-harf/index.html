## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long do led letter signs last?

The lifespan of led modules is generally between 50,000 and 100,000 hours. Led letter signs made with quality materials can be used trouble-free for many years.

What is the energy consumption of led letter signs?

Led technology is known for its low energy consumption. Led letter signs consume much less energy than traditional neon signs.

How do you maintain led letter signs?

Maintaining led letter signs is quite easy. Regular cleaning and replacing faulty led modules when necessary are sufficient.
