Professional Textile Printing Services in Gaziantep

As Reklam Atölyesi (Advertising Workshop), we offer professional textile printing solutions in Gaziantep. With our sublimation printing technology, direct textile printing systems, and transfer printing units, we can print on all kinds of fabrics. We provide high-quality printing services for t-shirts, jerseys, flags, banners, tablecloths, and textile promotional products.

Textile Printing Technologies

In our textile printing services, we use sublimation printing, direct textile printing, and transfer printing technologies. Sublimation printing is ideal for polyester fabrics and has high wash resistance. Direct textile printing is suitable for cotton and blended fabrics. Transfer printing is ideal for small quantities and custom designs.

Our digital textile printing machines, special textile inks, and professional fixing systems allow us to obtain prints with high wash resistance. We manufacture in accordance with corporate standards with Pantone color matching, detailed quality control, and professional packaging services.

Application Areas and Products

Our textile printing services offer a wide range of applications. We print on textiles for t-shirts, polo shirts, shirts, jerseys, flags, banners, tablecloths, curtains, and textile promotional products. We offer professional solutions for corporate textile products, promotional textiles, and custom-designed textiles.

We can print on cotton, polyester, knitted fabrics, and blended fabrics. We select the appropriate printing technique for different fabric types and achieve high-quality results.
