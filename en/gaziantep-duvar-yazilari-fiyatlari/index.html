What are the wall writing materials?

Generally, foil, vinyl, transparent printing, and similar materials are used in wall writing applications. The material selection is made according to the type of design and the surface to which it will be applied.

How do you clean wall writing?

Wall writing is usually cleaned with warm water and mild detergent. However, avoid using hard brushes or abrasive cleaning materials.
