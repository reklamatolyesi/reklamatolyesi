Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the lifespan of RGB LED letters?

The lifespan of RGB LED letters can vary depending on the quality of the LED modules used and external factors. However, quality LED modules have an average lifespan of 50,000 - 100,000 hours.

What should I pay attention to when cleaning RGB LED letters?

When cleaning RGB LED letters, use a soft cloth and mild soapy water. Avoid using abrasive cleaners or hard brushes, as they may damage the surface of the letters.

Can I use RGB LED letters outdoors?

Yes, RGB LED letters are designed for outdoor use. However, ensure that the letters are water and dust resistant. As Reklam Atölyesi, we produce durable LED letters suitable for outdoor conditions.

What should I consider during the design process?

During the design process, take care to create a design that reflects your business’s brand identity, is eye-catching, and is readable. Our Reklam Atölyesi design team will be happy to assist you in this regard.
