Box Letter Types and Options

There are different options available for plexiglass box letters:

  • Internally Illuminated Box Letters: These letters, illuminated with LED lights from inside the plexiglass, provide an impressive look in nighttime visibility.
  • Externally Illuminated Box Letters: Spotlights placed around the letters provide eye-catching illumination.
  • Surface Illuminated Box Letters: Light emanating from behind the plexiglass surface provides homogeneous illumination.
  • Stainless Steel Letter Combinations: You can combine plexiglass letters with stainless steel letters to achieve a richer look.
