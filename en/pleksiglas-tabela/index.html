How durable is a plexiglass sign?

Plexiglass is a very durable material against external factors. It is resistant to UV rays, water, and scratches. However, proper maintenance is important to extend its lifespan.

How do I clean a plexiglass sign?

Plexiglass signs can be easily cleaned with warm water and soapy cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners.
