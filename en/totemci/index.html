What to Consider in Totem Design?

  • Visual Appeal: It is important to use attractive and memorable visuals that are compatible with your brand's logo, colors and overall identity in totem design.
  • Readability: The text on the totem must be easily readable from a distance. Therefore, correct font selection and text sizes are of great importance.
  • Durability: Totems must be made of durable materials against external factors (sun, rain, wind, etc.) for long-lasting use.
  • Positioning: Correct positioning of the totem is important to attract the attention of the target audience and convey an effective advertising message.
