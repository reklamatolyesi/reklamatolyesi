## Gaziantep Professional Outdoor Wayfinding Systems

As Reklam Atölyesi (Advertising Workshop), we manufacture professional outdoor wayfinding systems in Gaziantep. Unlike illuminated signs, we focus on direction and information. While illuminated signs focus on lighting technology and brand visibility, outdoor wayfinding systems are designed for direction, information, and guidance. We offer durable and effective wayfinding solutions for large areas such as sites, campuses, parks, and industrial zones. We manufacture using outdoor-resistant materials such as aluminum composite, stainless steel, and galvanized sheet.

### Outdoor Wayfinding Features

Our outdoor wayfinding systems provide day and night visibility with reflective foils, LED lighting systems, and solar-powered solutions. We design direction signs, road signs, and park markings for site, campus, and park wayfinding systems. We carry out long-lasting applications with UV-resistant prints, special coating techniques, and anti-vandal systems.
