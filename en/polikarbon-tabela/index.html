## What are the Advantages of Polycarbonate Signage?

Polycarbonate signs have many advantages compared to traditional signage materials. Their lightweight nature makes them easy to install and reduces transportation costs. They are also resistant to external factors due to their high impact resistance. The transparent structure of polycarbonate, combined with interior lighting, creates a striking visual effect and ensures your brand is visible even at night. At Reklam Atölyesi, we work diligently to provide our customers in Gaziantep with the most suitable polycarbonate signage solutions.

### Why Choose Polycarbonate Signage?
  • Durability: Polycarbonate is resistant to breakage and scratches.
  • Lightweight: Easy to install and does not create structural load.
  • Light Translucency: Provides an impressive look with interior lighting.
  • Aesthetics: Offers a modern and stylish appearance.
  • Long-lasting: Maintains its original appearance for years.
