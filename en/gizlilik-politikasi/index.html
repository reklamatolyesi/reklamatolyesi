Privacy Policy

When you visit our website, some information is collected through cookies to improve your use of the site and the services we offer. This information, excluding your identity information, is used to better deliver our services and improve the user experience.

The collected information is not shared with third parties and is used only to improve the performance of our site and enhance our services. Users can prevent the use of cookies by changing their browser settings.

Your privacy is extremely important to us. We take the necessary technical and administrative measures to ensure the security of your personal information. If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us.

Cookie Usage and Data Collection

Cookies are used on our website to improve user experience and increase site performance. Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. These cookies are used to remember your preferences within the site, optimize page loading times, and provide you with a better experience.

The types of cookies used include session cookies, persistent cookies, and third-party cookies. Session cookies are automatically deleted when the browser is closed. Persistent cookies remain in your browser for a certain period and are used when you revisit the site. Third-party cookies are used for site analytics and advertising services.

Protection of Personal Data

As Reklam Atölyesi, we are extremely sensitive about the protection of your personal data. Within the scope of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), your personal data is processed only for the specified purposes and within legal limits. Collected personal data is only accessible by authorized personnel and is stored on secure servers.

Your personal data is processed to improve our service quality, fulfill our legal obligations, and manage our customer relationships. Your data is not shared with third parties without your consent and is used only for legal requirements and service quality improvements.

Data Security and Technical Measures

We take the necessary technical and administrative measures to ensure the security of your personal information. Data transmission is encrypted with an SSL certificate and stored on secure servers. Regular security updates are made, and protection against unauthorized access is provided.

We continuously improve data security and use the latest security technologies. We provide regular security training to our personnel and act in accordance with ethical rules in data processing processes. In the event of a data breach, notification is made to the relevant authorities within the legal period and users are informed.

User Rights and Communication

Under the KVKK, you have various rights regarding your personal data. You can submit requests for access, correction, deletion, or anonymization of your data. You can also request information about the purpose of processing your data and sharing it with third parties.

For any questions, requests, or complaints regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us. You can reach us through our communication channels to convey all your requests regarding your personal data. Your requests will be evaluated and answered as soon as possible.
