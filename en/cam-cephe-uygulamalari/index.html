Why Glass Facades? Advantages and Application Areas

Glass facades are a modern architectural solution frequently preferred in commercial and residential projects today. This preference stems from the numerous advantages they offer. By allowing maximum natural light to enter, they reduce lighting costs and create a more vibrant atmosphere in indoor spaces. At the same time, they block out external noise, providing a comfortable living or working environment. As Reklam Atölyesi, we carry out glass facade applications in various spaces in Gaziantep, such as office buildings, shopping centers, stores, hotels, and private residences.

Types of Glass Facade Systems

Glass facade systems are available in various types to meet different needs and design preferences. Reklam Atölyesi offers a wide range of options to provide the most suitable solution for each project:

  • Frameless Glass Facades: Offer a minimalist and elegant look. Particularly preferred in modern and luxurious projects.
  • Framed Glass Facades: A more economical option and provides structural support.
  • Semi-Structural Glass Facades: Offer a balanced solution in terms of both aesthetics and functionality.
  • Custom Design Glass Facades: We offer fully customized glass facade solutions according to our customers’ requests. With our designs that adapt to the unique architecture of Gaziantep, we make a difference.

Glass Facade Materials and Technologies

The quality and performance of glass facades are directly dependent on the materials and technologies used. As Reklam Atölyesi, we use the highest standard materials to offer long-lasting and reliable solutions. By using various types of glass such as thermally insulated glass, low-emissivity (low-e) glass, solar control glass, and laminated glass, we increase energy efficiency and enhance the comfort of spaces. We also ensure structural safety by using high-strength aluminum profiles and stainless steel connecting elements. We design durable and long-lasting glass facade systems suitable for the climate conditions of Gaziantep.
