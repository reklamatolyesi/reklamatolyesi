Personal Data

Your personal data is protected under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). Your data is processed only to improve our service quality and fulfill our legal obligations.

The personal data collected is accessible only by authorized personnel and will not be shared with third parties without your consent. You have the right to request access, correction, deletion, or anonymization of your data.

You can contact us for your requests regarding your personal data. The security and privacy of your data are our priority.

Data Processing Within the Scope of KVKK

As Reklam Atölyesi, we fulfill our legal obligations regarding the processing, protection and security of your personal data within the scope of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). Your personal data is processed only for the specified purposes and within legal limits.

The personal data collected includes name, surname, phone number, email address, address information and company information. This data is processed to improve our service quality, manage customer relationships, fulfill our legal obligations and improve our services.

Data Security and Access Control

Your personal data is accessible only by authorized personnel and is stored on secure servers. Data access logs are kept and protection against unauthorized access is provided. Data transmission is encrypted with an SSL certificate and regular security updates are performed.

Your data will not be shared with third parties without your consent and will only be used for legal obligations and service quality improvements. In the event of data sharing, legal procedures are followed and necessary security measures are taken.

Data Subject Rights

Within the scope of KVKK, you have various rights regarding your personal data. You may request access, correction, deletion or anonymization of your data. You can also request information about the purpose of processing your data, the duration of processing, sharing with third parties and data security measures.

You can reach us through our communication channels to exercise your data subject rights. Your requests will be evaluated and answered within a maximum of 30 days. Your data deletion requests will be evaluated taking into account the legal retention periods.

Data Processing Periods and Storage

Your personal data is processed for the specified purposes and stored for the legal retention periods. In the event of termination of the service relationship, your data will be deleted or anonymized taking into account the legal retention periods.

Data security and privacy are our priority. We are constantly improving the protection of your personal data and using the latest security technologies. In the event of a data breach, the relevant authorities will be notified within the legal period and data subjects will be informed.
