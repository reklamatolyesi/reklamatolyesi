How to Maintain Plexiglass Signage?

Regular maintenance is important to ensure the longevity of your plexiglass signs. Here’s what you need to pay attention to:

  • Clean your signs with a soft cloth and soapy water.
  • Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.
  • Protect the surface of your signs from scratches.
  • Regularly check the lighting system of your LED signs.
