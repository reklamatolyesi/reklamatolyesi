Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the fair stand design process work?

First, we learn your requests and expectations. Then, we develop a concept suitable for your brand identity and products. After the concept is approved, we prepare 3D visual designs and present them to you for your approval. After the approved design, we start the production of the stand and carry out its installation at the fairground.

What types of advertising materials can you produce?

We can produce brochures, flyers, catalogs, posters, banners, roll-ups, brochure holders, tablecloths, hats, t-shirts and all other promotional products. We can also create custom designs according to your needs.

Do you have post-fair support services?

Yes, we also have post-fair support services. We can analyze the information you obtain from fair visitors and help you develop your future marketing strategies. In addition, we can provide you with support for the dismantling and storage of your fair stand.
