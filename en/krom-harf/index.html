Chrome Letter Production Process

At Reklam Atölyesi, chrome letter production is carried out with a meticulous process. First, the design approved by the customer is prepared in a computer environment. Then, the stainless chrome materials are cut, bent and welded. The surface of the letters is smoothed and painted in the desired color. Finally, the letters are prepared for installation and delivered to your address within or outside of Gaziantep. With our principle of fast delivery, we complete your projects on time.

Gaziantep Chrome Letter Care and Cleaning

Chrome letters can maintain their original shine for many years with regular maintenance and cleaning. Regularly wiping the surface of the letters with a damp cloth prevents dust and dirt from accumulating. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. To prevent rusting of the chrome letters, a protective layer can be applied, especially in coastal or humid environments. As Reklam Atölyesi, we can also provide you with support regarding chrome letter care and cleaning.
