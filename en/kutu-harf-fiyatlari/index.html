How long can box letter signs last?

The lifespan of box letter signs depends on the material quality, weather conditions, and maintenance. Box letter signs made with quality materials and regularly maintained can last for many years.

How are box letter signs installed?

The installation of box letter signs should be done by an experienced team. It is important that the sign is mounted securely and safely during installation.

How should box letter signs be maintained?

Maintenance of box letter signs is done by regular cleaning and repairing damaged parts. It is especially important to regularly check the lights of LED illuminated signs and replace faulty modules.
