### Design Tips

Here are some important points to consider when designing a sidewalk sign:

  1. Brand Identity: Ensure the design of your sign reflects your business’s brand identity.
  2. Logo and Colors: Use your business’s logo and colors on your sign to increase brand awareness.
  3. Readability: Make sure the text on your sign is easy to read from a distance.
  4. Lighting: Choose illuminated signs to increase nighttime visibility.
  5. Location: Ensure the location of your sign is suitable in terms of pedestrian and vehicle traffic.
