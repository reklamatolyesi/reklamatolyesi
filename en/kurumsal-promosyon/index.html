Gaziantep Protocol and Management Level Corporate Gift Sets

As Reklam Atölyesi (Advertising Workshop), we design corporate gift sets in Gaziantep that stand out from classic promotional products, focusing entirely on protocol, board of directors, and high-level guests. This page covers limited-edition, personalized, and prestige-focused special series products, not textile promotions (t-shirts, hats, etc.) or office promotions (agendas, pens, notepads, etc.).

Limited Production Corporate Gift Concepts

In our corporate gift projects, we create a brand-specific concept each year or for each campaign. For example, we prepare numbered production crystal plaques for board members, leather agenda-pen sets engraved with dates and names for company anniversary celebrations, and special boxed sets with country and name printing for foreign delegation visits. Each product is made unique with a serial number, personal name engraving, and date information.

This approach is completely different from bulk textile promotions for distribution purposes. The aim here is to produce a small number of gifts with high perceived value, which will be kept for a long time and displayed in showcases.

Personalized Naming and Signature Applications

In corporate gift sets, we offer full personalization with recipient name, title, date, and, if desired, handwritten signature applications. We use high-level techniques such as laser engraving, chemical marking on metal, sandblasting on glass and crystal, printing and embossing on leather. If desired, the chairman of the board's signature, message, or company motto can be added to the set as a card, certificate, or metal plate.

Unlike office promotions, here the commemorative value and the prestige it represents are more important than the product's daily use. Therefore, we prefer premium materials such as brass, crystal, genuine leather, and solid wood in material selection.

Protocol and Special Guest Scenarios

We create gift concepts for municipalities, chambers, associations, industrial organizations, and large-scale companies for special occasions such as foreign guests, delegation visits, opening ceremonies, protocol invitations, and signing ceremonies. For each scenario, we offer different content considering the target audience, cultural sensitivities, and budget. For example, we design special objects reflecting Turkish and Gaziantep culture for international delegations, and more corporate and branded sets for local business partners.

Project-Based Planning and Limited Quantity Production

In corporate gift projects, we generally prefer limited-quantity, project-based production instead of stocking and unlimited production. This makes the gifts more valuable and special for the recipients. At the beginning of the project, we finalize the quantity, delivery date, name lists, and personalization details, and plan production and packaging accordingly.

If you want to develop management-level and protocol-focused corporate gift concepts for institutions in Gaziantep and surrounding cities, we conduct a consultancy-based study where we can plan the entire process together, from design to delivery.

Special Box Design and Presentation

One of the most important elements determining the perception of corporate gift sets is the presentation. For this reason, we design gift boxes with magnetic lids, internal compartments, foam inserts, or velvet beds, specifically for each project's content. We complete the presentation by adding certificate cards, thank-you notes, or personalized message cards to the boxes.

This is exactly what sets us apart from our textile promotion pages: the products we discuss here are not for trade show giveaways; they are high-value pieces displayed on protocol tables, in office showcases, and in executive offices.
