Vehicle Advertising Types in Gaziantep

As Reklam Atölyesi, we offer a variety of vehicle advertising types tailored to different needs in Gaziantep. This allows you to deliver your brand’s message most effectively.

Full Vehicle Wrap

You can achieve maximum visibility with full vehicle wraps. Our design team creates unique designs that reflect your brand’s identity in Gaziantep. This method turns your vehicles into moving billboards, attracting great attention.

Partial Vehicle Wrap

We offer a more economical solution by wrapping certain parts of your vehicles. The sides and rear of vehicles are ideal areas for conveying your message. It is an effective method to emphasize your brand name and logo in Gaziantep.

Vehicle Graphics (One-Way Vision)

One-way vision films are applied to vehicle windows and contain advertisements that are clearly visible from the outside. However, they do not obstruct the driver’s view from the inside. This professional application ensures your brand attracts attention in Gaziantep traffic.
