### The Importance and Functions of Traffic Signs

Traffic signs are vital for regulating road traffic, informing drivers, and ensuring safety. Properly positioned and understandable traffic signs help prevent accidents and improve traffic flow. As **Reklam Atölyesi**, we ensure that **Gaziantep**’s traffic signs comply with current regulations and are easily readable.

#### Types of Traffic Signs
  • **Warning Signs:** Inform drivers of dangerous situations such as sharp curves, narrow roads, and pedestrian crossings.
  • **Traffic Regulation Signs:** Contain information such as speed limits, right-of-way rules, and directions.
  • **Information Signs:** Provide information such as distance, settlements, and tourist attractions.
  • **Direction Signs:** Guide to places in different directions.
