Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between router engraving and laser engraving?

Router engraving is typically used to create three-dimensional designs on thicker materials, while laser engraving is ideal for finer and more detailed designs. Laser engraving is more precise and sharp.

What materials are suitable for router engraving and laser engraving?

Wood, acrylic, PVC and aluminum are suitable for router engraving. Laser engraving can be used on a wide variety of materials including glass, acrylic, leather, metal, and more.
