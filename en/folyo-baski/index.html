Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is foil printing?

Foil printing is a printing technique that involves applying special foil materials to various surfaces. It is often used for advertising, promotion, and decorative purposes.

How durable is foil printing?

The durability of foil printing depends on the quality of the foil material used and the surface to which it is applied. Quality foil materials are resistant to weather conditions and can be used for a long time without fading.

How long does foil printing application take?

The duration of foil printing application varies depending on the size, complexity, and surface of the print. It usually can be completed within a few hours.
