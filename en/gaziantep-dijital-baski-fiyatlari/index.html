Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is digital printing?

Digital printing is the process of transferring designs prepared in a computer environment directly to various materials through a printing machine. It is faster, more flexible, and more economical compared to traditional printing methods.

On what materials can digital printing be performed?

Digital printing can be performed on vinyl, tarps, paper, fabric, plexiglass, wood, and many other materials. Reklam Atölyesi achieves the best results by using suitable printing techniques for different materials.
