Plexi box letters are three-dimensional letters made from plexi material. These letters are commonly used to create logos, names, or slogans for businesses. What are the Advantages of Plexi Box Letters?

  • Aesthetic Appearance: Plexi can be produced in transparent, colored, or matte finishes, allowing you to achieve a look that suits your brand's image.
  • Durability: Plexi is highly resistant to external factors and maintains its original appearance for years.
  • Lightweight: Being lighter than other materials, plexi is easy to install and poses a lower risk of damage to buildings.
  • Variety: Plexi box letters can be produced in different thicknesses, colors, and sizes, allowing you to maintain design freedom.
  • Lighting Options: Plexi box letters can be illuminated from the inside or outside, creating a striking appearance even at night.

Plexi box letters are highly resistant to external factors and maintain their original appearance for years. We use UV-resistant plexi materials to prevent fading.

How are Plexi Box Letters Installed?
