Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long do chrome signs last?

Chrome signs can maintain their original appearance for many years with proper maintenance. The stainless steel material is durable against external factors and does not corrode.

How should I maintain my chrome signs?

You can clean your chrome signs regularly by wiping them with a damp cloth. Avoid harsh cleaning agents and do not use abrasive substances.
