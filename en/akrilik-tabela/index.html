## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Acrylic is a UV-resistant and weather-resistant material. Acrylic signs produced with correct production techniques maintain their appearance for many years.

How do I clean my acrylic signs?

You can clean your acrylic signs with a slightly damp cloth. Avoid using abrasive cleaning materials.

When designing a sign, be sure to create a design that reflects your business’s logo, colors, and identity. It is also important that the sign is readable and eye-catching.
