## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does the product photography service take?

The shooting time varies depending on the number of products and the environment in which the shooting will take place. Generally, a shooting session can take from a few hours to a day.

In what format will you deliver the photos?

We usually deliver photos in JPEG and PNG formats in high resolution. We can also deliver them in different formats upon your request.

Is a studio required for the shoot?

As Reklam Atölyesi, we can shoot in our own studio or, upon your request, in different locations. The location selection is determined according to the type of product and the shooting concept.

How is the photo editing done?

Color corrections, contrast adjustments, retouching, and other edits of the photos are done using professional photo editing software. During the editing process, the goal is to make the products look their best.
