How Do I Maintain Golden Lettering?

Maintaining golden lettering is quite easy. You can clean it regularly by wiping it with a damp cloth. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. This way, you can preserve the brightness and appearance of the letters for years to come.
